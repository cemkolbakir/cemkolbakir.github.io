Research Interests

  • Plasma aerodynamics: thermal and momentum effects of AC- and nanosecond-pulsed DBD plasma actuators
  • Icing mitigation: plasma-based anti-/de-icing for aircraft and wind turbines, hybrid strategies with superhydrophobic coatings
  • Flow control: separation, wake and gust control on airfoils at low Reynolds numbers
  • Experimental methods: flow visualization, hot-wire anemometry, force and infrared measurements

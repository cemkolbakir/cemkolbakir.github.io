Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Who we are

CoachKonnekt is a private coaching tool based in Türkiye. It lets a coach see the WHOOP data of athletes who choose to connect their account, so training can be adjusted to each athlete's recovery and sleep.

The app is not a medical service and is not affiliated with WHOOP, Inc. Questions about this policy: cem@kolbakir.com.

Data we collect

We only receive data after an athlete signs in to WHOOP and approves access. The athlete sees the exact permissions on WHOOP's own screen.

How we use it and who sees it

The data is used only to show each athlete's metrics and trends to their coach and to plan training. Only the athlete's coach on CoachKonnekt and the athlete themself can see an athlete's data.

We do not use the data for advertising, marketing, profiling for other purposes, or automated decisions about the athlete. Athletes on the same team cannot see each other's data unless they agree in writing.

Storage, security and retention

Data is stored on a server we control, hosted by Supabase on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union). Connections use encrypted HTTPS, and access tokens are stored encrypted. Only the coach can log in to the dashboard.

We keep an athlete's data while they are connected and for as long as they train with the coach. When an athlete disconnects or asks for deletion, we delete their data and tokens within 30 days.

Sharing

We never sell, rent or license athlete data. We do not share it with third parties, except:

  • our hosting provider (Supabase), which stores the data on our behalf and cannot use it for its own purposes
  • when the law requires it
  • with another person, such as a team doctor or physiotherapist, only with the athlete's explicit consent

Your rights

Connecting is voluntary, and athletes can stop at any time. Under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, No. 6698) and, where it applies, the EU GDPR, athletes can:

  • see what data we hold about them and get a copy
  • correct or delete their data
  • withdraw consent, either by emailing us or by removing CoachKonnekt in their WHOOP account settings
  • complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or their local authority

We answer requests within 30 days.

Minors

Athletes under 18 may connect only with written consent from a parent or legal guardian.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and tell connected athletes before the change takes effect.

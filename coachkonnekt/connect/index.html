Connect your WHOOP

Your coach uses CoachKonnekt to plan training around how recovered you are. Connecting takes about a minute.

  • Your coach sees your recovery, sleep, strain, workouts and body measurements. Nobody else does.
  • You can disconnect at any time in the WHOOP app, under connected apps.
